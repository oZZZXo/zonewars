ZONE WARS: PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 2026-11-15

In short

Zone Wars has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. We do not sell or share your data.

Stays on your device

Your campaign progress, records, settings, replays and custom maps are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Online play

Only when you use online multiplayer, the game connects to our relay server, which passes messages between the players of a room. To do this the relay sees your IP address and handles:

  • the display name you chose (you can pick any name, no real name is needed),
  • room information (the 4-letter room code, who is in the room),
  • game messages between players in the room, and
  • a random token generated on your device, used by the host to recognise you if you reconnect to a running match. It is not linked to you and is not shared with anyone but the host of that match.

The relay keeps this only in memory while you are connected. It does not store it in a database or file. Its log contains only a periodic status line with totals (number of rooms, connections and messages), not names, IP addresses or message content. Nothing is kept once the connection closes. There is no chat; other players in the room see your display name.

Same Wi-Fi (LAN) play

Data goes directly between the devices on your local network. It does not pass through our server.

Security

Online connections are encrypted in transit (wss / TLS).

Children

Zone Wars is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
